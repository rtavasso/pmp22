# P1-selective promoter modulation

- **Priority:** 3
- **Direction:** Reduce P1 initiation while preserving P2 and cell identity.
- **Decisive experiment:** Promoter-resolved CRISPRi titration with long-read or validated junction assays, total PMP22 RNA, protein, and matched P2/non-targeting controls.

This hypothesis tests whether promoter choice permits more selective control
than broad locus repression.  FANTOM donor files and promoter intervals require
final QC and coordinate freezing. A CAGE or accessibility prediction is not an
expected percentage protein change.
