# State-specific distal element

- **Priority:** 4
- **Direction:** Perturb a human-supported element active in a myelinating or repair state and predict a state-dependent PMP22 response.
- **Decisive experiment:** Crossed donor-by-state CRISPRi design with identical guides and early chromatin/RNA plus later protein/function readouts.

Candidate selection is blocked on donor-resolved state data.  The design must
cross study and state; otherwise a state effect is not identifiable. A result
that tracks broad loss of Schwann identity rather than local perturbation is a
failed selectivity hypothesis.
