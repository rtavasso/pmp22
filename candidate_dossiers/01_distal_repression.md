# Distal enhancer cluster repression

- **Priority:** 1
- **Direction:** Repress candidate distal activity; expect lower native PMP22 if necessary.
- **Decisive experiment:** Tiled CRISPRi in donor-resolved human Schwann cells with PMP22 total/P1/P2 RNA, protein, neighbor-gene, identity, viability, and non-targeting controls.

**Evidence.** Published rodent distal regulatory and injury-state evidence makes
this a positive-control family, but the exact human ortholog and native human
effect remain unresolved.

**Discrimination.** A local chromatin change followed by selective PMP22 change,
without early broad myelin/identity loss, supports direct regulation.  General
identity loss or many neighboring-gene effects argues against selectivity.

**Not demonstrated.** No therapeutic effect, safe dose, precise protein change,
or native human necessity is claimed.
