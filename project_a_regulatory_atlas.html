Scientific review and revised scope

This revision distinguishes confirmed factual/metadata corrections, under-specified methods and unresolved evidence. The project is viable only for the claim level its measurements identify. The detailed corrections below supplement and, where narrower, supersede earlier broad framing.

Problem statement and research contract

Objective: construct an auditable map of DNA elements that regulate PMP22, determine which evidence transfers to human Schwann cells and their different states, and nominate a small set of selective native-regulation hypotheses. The primary output is a ranked, evidence-backed experimental dossier—not an unvalidated therapeutic sequence.

The relevant disease contrast is PMP22-dosage CMT1A versus deletion-HNPP. The coding sequence can remain intact while expression changes: distal-regulatory deletion has altered endogenous Pmp22 and produced neuropathy-relevant phenotypes in experimental systems. This provides a mechanistic foundation for studying noncoding control, but does not establish the effect of every human orthologous interval.[1],[8]

Contract itemDefinition for this project
Primary questionWhich regulatory intervals have reproducible, state-relevant evidence for changing native PMP22 expression, and which proposed sequence or activity changes are most informative to test?
Primary estimandFor an existing perturbation assay: change in its measured outcome under the intervention versus its appropriate control. For observational data: reproducible locus activity or occupancy, not a causal expression effect.
Unit of evidenceA source experiment with a defined species, donor or tissue pool, cell state, intervention, assay and control. Multiple tracks derived from one source are not independent experiments.
Desired generalizationFrom characterized rodent and reporter elements to the relevant human Schwann-cell state; from sequence prediction to held-out experimental effects only where those effects are available.
Out of scopeClaiming clinical efficacy; choosing a patient-specific dose; treating open chromatin as an expression effect; training a general regulatory model solely on a handful of PMP22 elements.

Biological orientation and established controls

A promoter initiates transcription; an enhancer is a regulatory DNA element whose effect depends on the cell and genomic context. PMP22 has major promoter-associated noncoding first exons, P1/exon 1A and P2/exon 1B, that feed into common protein-coding exons. A regulatory change can therefore affect total output, promoter choice, or both. Analyze these separately.[1],[2]

Schwann cells are the glia that support and myelinate peripheral axons. Developing, mature myelinating and injury/repair-associated cells are not interchangeable regulatory contexts. SOX10 and EGR2 supply important mechanistic priors; TEAD1 is a DNA-binding factor connected to YAP/TAZ cofactor activity. A perturbation of this broader program can change PMP22 indirectly while changing many other myelin genes.[4],[3],[7]

The first acquisition milestone

Start with the rat GEO catalogs, published PMP22 perturbation evidence and verified FANTOM donor metadata. Resolve the human ATAC source and model-exposure graph before making biological generalization claims. Download only after verifying file identity, QC and permitted uses.

Analysis plan and acceptance gates

Freeze a locus evidence dictionary

Define the gene-oriented locus, promoter-specific first exons, known intronic/distal elements, neighboring genes and plausible broader regulatory boundaries. Reconstruct historical intervals from original sequence where possible. Convert each assay into a typed evidence record: binding, accessibility, histone activity, reporter response, native RNA response, protein response or function. Do not collapse these into one “enhancer score.”

Extract effects with their original scale and denominator; digitized figures must be labeled as such and independently checked. Include controls and negative mutations, not only positive regions. Human hg18 intervals in the distal-enhancer paper require remapping, not relabeling as GRCh38.[2],[1]

Acceptance gate. Every known region has source, assembly, coordinate convention, sequence or retrieval route, tested construct and evidence type. Multi-mapped or unresolvable regions are flagged rather than forced into the map.

Build sample, source-overlap and permission manifests

Record donor, tissue pool, library, run, assay, biological state, perturbation and control separately. Build a source-overlap graph: GEO deposit → publication → derived atlas → pretrained model target. This distinguishes independent validation from reprocessed or repackaged training material.

Keep an explicit exposure field for each model/input pair: known training exposure, known held-out source, or unknown. Borzoi’s inventory makes several potential exposures directly testable.[21],[22]

Acceptance gate. No experiment is counted twice through derived resources. No cells, strands, sequencing runs or IP inputs are counted as independent donors. Every restricted resource has an authorized access route.

Reproduce known locus observations before fitting models

Use processed tracks first, then consistent raw reprocessing only where it adds value. Inspect sequencing depth, signal-to-background, peak reproducibility, mappability, library complexity and relevant assay-specific QC. Compare nerve and CNS/repair contexts only with the available biological replication and technical differences made explicit.

The rat H3K27ac deposit is useful descriptive evidence, but cannot supply a replicated differential test by treating its input as a second specimen.[6]

Acceptance gate. The known regions are located consistently, obvious assay failures are documented, and discrepancies with published figures are resolved or retained in a discrepancy log.

Separate human evidence from cross-species transfer

For each rodent element, align the underlying sequence and flanks to the human locus, record one-to-many and unmapped relationships, and compare motifs and human activity rather than assuming conservation of function. Inspect human cell composition; use cell-specific data where available and label bulk nerve as bulk.

A proposed cell-state contrast should include uncertainty from donor and differentiation stage. If raw donor-resolved accessibility is unavailable, make a descriptive atlas instead of claiming a cell-state classifier has been externally validated.

Acceptance gate. Each candidate has a human-support status and an orthology confidence note. Missing human evidence remains a missing-data label, not a negative functional result.

Benchmark models against the measurement they predict

Compare simple baselines—distance to promoter, motifs, conservation and measured accessibility—with ChromBPNet, Borzoi or AlphaGenome only for suitable outputs. Match strand, assembly, input window and assay head. For a model-supported perturbation claim, use held-out experimental mutation/deletion outcomes where available; otherwise call it a prediction of observational signal.[23],[22],[24]

Use chromosome/region-block splits for sequence similarity leakage and donor/study splits for biological generalization. Neither erases pretraining exposure. Keep PMP22 nomination separate from model choice; a few dozen related mutations in one enhancer are not a large independent test set.

Acceptance gate. The benchmark states its effective number of independent elements and studies, baseline comparisons, uncertainty and pretraining limitations. No model is selected solely because its PMP22 importance plot looks plausible.

Nominate discriminating native interventions

Rank candidates by the kind and relevance of evidence, not an opaque sum presented as a probability of therapeutic success. Prefer a compact panel containing known positive controls, high-confidence human candidates, a state-specific hypothesis and matched negative controls. State what result would distinguish direct PMP22 regulation from general loss of Schwann identity.

For each nomination, specify direction, predicted assay-level effect, plausible off-target/neighbor effects, and whether repression, activation or sequence editing is the appropriate mechanistic test. Pass the dossier to Project D; pass promoter/UTR ambiguity to Project B.

Acceptance gate. Each candidate is a falsifiable question with a measurement plan and an explicit uncertainty statement. Predicted accessibility changes are never converted into an unsupported percentage change in PMP22 protein.

Operational safeguards for the atlas and benchmark

The strongest objection: an atlas assembled from known enhancers, pooled tracks and pretrained-model scores can reproduce the literature without demonstrating new human regulation. The revised objective is a precisely bounded resource or benchmark first; a new causal regulator is a separate claim.

Checkpoint-specific evaluation, rather than blanket exclusion

A pooled human accessibility profile can support a provisional within-profile genomic model if coverage and held-out regions are adequate. It cannot identify cross-donor response variation or a population-level state effect. Conversely, another donor from a training tissue does not by itself certify a novel-locus test. Treat those cases separately.

For the standard Borzoi checkpoints, the official split specification is test fold3 and validation fold4. The four model replicates share these splits; they are not four independent biological validations. AlphaGenome’s research quick-start loads all_folds, which is not evidence of a genomic holdout. Inspect a checkpoint’s actual exposure before using it as a benchmark.

Predeclared benchmark report

Write benchmark_contract.tsv before scoring PMP22: task, input-information tier, test unit, positive/negative ascertainment, reference prevalence, output transform, primary metric, uncertainty procedure and validation scope. For candidate classification report precision–recall against its prevalence baseline; for profiles separate counts from shape; for measured mutations compare effect direction and scale to the matching assay. Rank correlations alone can hide bad calibration.

Perform reverse-complement and genomic-coordinate tests, shifts of the input window, annotation-version checks, and a label-permutation control. Treat agreement among related models as robustness evidence, not independent biological corroboration. An unavailable held-out test is a limitation to report, not a reason to relabel training fit as discovery.

Do not confuse selectivity with an unchanged disease transcriptome

PMP22-focused correction can produce downstream normalization of a broader disease program. Therefore, unchanged expression of every myelin-related gene is not a necessary definition of selective action. Prespecify which early local effects would indicate direct off-target regulation, which later changes are expected consequences of rescue, and which changes indicate identity loss or toxicity. Keep those endpoints separate.

Proposed primary benchmark metrics should follow the labels: precision–recall for highly imbalanced candidate classification, error/correlation for matched quantitative assay values, and effect-direction accuracy plus uncertainty for mutation outcomes. Cluster uncertainty at the independent element, donor or study level. A result that models fail to transfer to human Schwann biology is a useful outcome if the test is well specified.

Selectivity is an independent endpoint

Measure or predict PMP22 alongside neighboring genes and a broader myelin/cell-state panel. A candidate that reduces the whole differentiation program is not equivalent to a locus-selective intervention. Treat adjustment for cell state as a sensitivity analysis: state may be a confounder in observational comparisons and a mediator of the intervention, so blindly regressing it away can answer the wrong question.

Execution environment and remaining acquisition work

Use a processed-data-first route: inspect metadata, small locus slices and published tables before acquiring large read collections. Size storage and compute from actual byte counts and a representative pilot; the audit did not benchmark runtime or establish a required cloud budget. Keep large public alignments remote or selectively fetched where authorized.

A CPU workstation is sufficient for metadata reconciliation and initial locus summaries. Raw multi-study reprocessing benefits from workflow-managed batch/HPC compute; deep-model training or large-scale inference generally requires a suitable GPU or authorized API. Availability and license terms must be checked at execution, not assumed from a repository URL.

Pin genome assembly, annotation release, sample identifiers, workflow versions, container digests, model checkpoints and random seeds. Keep original downloads read-only and record URLs, checksums, timestamps, permissions and source-study citations. Never silently replace an unavailable input with a different tissue, species, release or derived copy of the same cohort.

Definition of doneA second researcher can reconstruct known regulatory evidence, tell which human measurements are genuinely independent, reproduce the benchmark, and execute a small, justified native-perturbation test without guessing the missing biological assumptions.

Scope and publication boundary

The primary disease scope is duplication-CMT1A and deletion-HNPP. Coding-variant neuropathies, including nominal loss-of-function alleles, need their own genotype-specific mechanism contract. This is a research design restriction, not treatment advice.

Call a result a replicated resource, a predictive benchmark, an association, a causal molecular effect or a functional rescue only when its stated evidence supports that category. Neither a negative finding nor a well-organized atlas is automatically publishable: novelty, information content, independent validation, accessible code/data and effect precision must justify the contribution. A narrow, well-supported result is preferable to an unsupported broad conclusion.

Sources marked unresolved or retained from the earlier handoff are not newly certified. The revised reference catalog separates earlier inspection from facts reverified here.
